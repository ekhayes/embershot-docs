**Step 1:** Click on the menu in the top left corner.

![](/img/help/uPWKld4t8D-8TEVzFBef88MEDGRnze5Rug-d01b9ef77103.png)

**Step 2:** From the dropdown, select 'Print.'

![](/img/help/M1W9RZtFa1Sxu2X8ExaNOP1GoFxRkgzDqQ-1696c6278773.png)

**Step 3:** Specify 'Print with annotations.'

![](/img/help/1rpHjGo1HhubSzDrDfzXQbRYspc5eggpsw-ef9950b13c3f.png)

**Step 4:** Specify desired printing conditions. Click 'Print.'

![](/img/help/t3PJcyWMCoV5uTLGAgWXDp2Be3IB37gMuw-1782938f7198.png)
