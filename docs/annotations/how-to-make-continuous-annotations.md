**Step 1:** When viewing a document, click on the menu in the top left corner.

![](/img/help/YeEpdss9__pw-V38DRa7C7E-41uWXHiXow-87061d7f4747.png)

**Step 2:** From the dropdown, select 'Annotating Options.' Make sure the toggle is on (orange) for 'Continuous Annotations.'

![](/img/help/ncrVnLkEycmhJDkj7x9DLEw-aVGo04Kw7Q-64a89dba25db.png)
