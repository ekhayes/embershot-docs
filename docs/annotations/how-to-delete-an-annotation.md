**Step 1:** Click the annotation you wish to delete. When the annotation is selected, an outline appears around it.

![](/img/help/6qMZ9ydauMu4QWJpPCK-J2UcSh1jpYWDfg-63710b321668.png)

**Step 2:** Click on the annotation dropdown.

![](/img/help/t00f3csOC5hZGGgBJ7XuW6ELaOCRrYg8WQ-cf4d039b077e.png)

**Step 3:** Click on the trash icon.

![](/img/help/CmB2u9iG8b7GQYVvGi-sdbU9IGSnDPb7hA-06eb6d8f276b.png)

**Tip:** With the annotation selected, you can also press **Delete** on your keyboard. Press **Esc** to let go of the annotation without deleting it.

You can only delete your own annotations. A permanent annotation can only be deleted by the file admin, and deleting it removes it for every reader.
