**Step 1:** Double click on the annotation you wish to delete. When the annotation is selected it will be highlighted in red.

![](/img/help/6qMZ9ydauMu4QWJpPCK-J2UcSh1jpYWDfg-63710b321668.png)

**Step 2:** Click on the annotation dropdown.

![](/img/help/t00f3csOC5hZGGgBJ7XuW6ELaOCRrYg8WQ-cf4d039b077e.png)

**Step 3:** Click on the trash icon.

![](/img/help/CmB2u9iG8b7GQYVvGi-sdbU9IGSnDPb7hA-06eb6d8f276b.png)
