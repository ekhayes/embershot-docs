**Step 1:** Click on the link icon in the toolbar

![](/img/help/3RskTTFXWxZnjZ7r7krqE6gENJCep0tluw-e08294ef7143.png)

**Step 2:** Highlight the desired text that you wish to turn into a link

![](/img/help/PTRiTsHIOzIJQjeHbbRB0Isan172_sZNhw-3e7ce8b8a514.png)

**Step 3:** Enter the URL in the text field and hit "Save"

![](/img/help/N3OErnVOBD8_AbFu0QtpsOUa5s8W9sdD_w-4118c34d8e50.png)
