---
**Step 1:** Click on the "tT" in the bottom left corner of the annotation box.

![](/img/help/jC7IPHq_v4OCYYjwuJiqgkGQuTZbX37i1A-1654a2d414e9.png)

**Step 2:** Drag the "tT" downwards to make the text bigger or upwards to make the text smaller.
