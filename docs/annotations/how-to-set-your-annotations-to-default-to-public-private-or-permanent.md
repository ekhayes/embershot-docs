**Step 1:** When viewing a document, click on the menu in the top left corner.

![](/img/help/YeEpdss9__pw-V38DRa7C7E-41uWXHiXow-87061d7f4747.png)

**Step 2:** From the dropdown, select 'Annotating Options' and change the toggle to the desired setting

![](/img/help/7HZkeepi6-vGA8Igq4J6tcECQ3jK6yY2_g-046561a780ef.png)

**Note:** This setting adjusts the "Default" only. To modify settings on an existing annotation, click on the annotation and adjust the setting directly.
