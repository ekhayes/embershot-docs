**Step 1:** Click the account icon in top right corner

![](/img/help/TASuJExuJfLDYeUkQc0tSf4Og1zySs0uIw-f88ff79ed6ae.png)

**Step 2:** Click on 'Account Settings'

![](/img/help/98hYu0ZUNuaZGBjYxmVHg-AOgvp1iLp7qA-6f29bdd00164.png)

**Step 3:** Click on 'Billing & Subscriptions'

![](/img/help/DAXnGCy136LlsgKMF8NPqA-2Bcfp73wllQ-68a8a72ab874.png)

**Step 4:** Click 'Upgrade Now'

![](/img/help/76KDgqnO65MVLUY0RybV3JfWLA-tby3HGQ-b09a207054b8.png)

**Step 5:** Enter payment info and select 'Subscribe'

![](/img/help/eslkauRxKGZTEuDpjQqodV4JHcHZFY6w8Q-570b24a01aa7.png)

**Step 6:** If you would like to then create a project you can do so by then clicking the 'Create Project' button

![](/img/help/DPbcCGJU92f5tIRFNaEyZldCErCo49Wg0Q-9cae7ee581fa.jpeg)
