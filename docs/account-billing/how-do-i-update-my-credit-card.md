**Step 1:** Click the account icon in top right corner

![](/img/help/klxvrVeSAO1_OZvk423BippnnyTyzAcV_g-9a34e497701e.jpeg)

**Step 2:** Click on 'Account Settings'

![](/img/help/crrk5WzjyxoXu78V1xsm7iFQgfJF-WgzSw-4bd0d792a9e6.jpeg)

**Step 3:** Click on 'Billing & Subscriptions'

![](/img/help/FyiP1glnzBsyN28PPFn1dS_-KxkkROug-g-e1ef9a9f5509.jpeg)

**Step 4:** Click on the edit icon next to the last four digits of your credit card

![](/img/help/jvUPOrlKQMx_o_jT-Q7R78TX4vPv75Nz7g-b11ba2ba1db6.png)

**Step 5:** Enter in your new credit card information and click "Update"

![](/img/help/yWQUwgnjtjpsZ8nJmcbYvn-FWJfc60DWlQ-7bb17ce30a0e.jpeg)
