**Step 1:** Log in to [embershot.com](//embershot.com) on your browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, etc).

**Step 2:** Click on the Account icon in the top right.

![](/img/help/Z8oe0pcA8ji5IDE3OCwGmJMsYbMYzLgZmA-00937864fdcd.png)

**Step 3:** Select "Account Settings" from the dropdown.

![](/img/help/UocFyXOxy2rrr1qg4-v7Cz0_OOBhjlJUWg-3cdb2f310611.png)

**Step 4:** Click on "Upgrade Now."

![](/img/help/zDXWJaBhrjQvTbTJYKKxCDJxYmLuILwb5A-cb63c6e17c17.png)

**Step 5:** Enter payment info and select "Subscribe."

![](/img/help/W5-cy5Qd6LtGs-rV8Qkj8r9-sHyA2Upcg-370ba3c56452.png)
