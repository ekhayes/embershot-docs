Pro, Project, and Enterprise Account holders can cancel their subscriptions by following the steps below.

Free Lite Account holders can contact [support@embershot.com](mailto:support@embershot.com), if they would like to request a complete deactivation and deletion of their account.

**Step 1:** Select the account icon in the top right corner

![](/img/help/GbDWQ2Ss7m4DwucOZSifhJbsm5P0vecsuQ-61aa24d61e55.png)

**Step 2:** Select 'Account Settings'

![](/img/help/A2rtXoBFLNnmvPajL0LP3M9XHbWld2Ikfw-633a662c10c5.png)

**Step 3:** Select the 'Billing & Subscription' tab

![](/img/help/xzjaEgiZLhK6-e7xmRAWNH8gJvGubvFwqA-3bf8110289a9.png)

**Step 4:** Click the 'Downgrade to Lite' button

![](/img/help/H8S9jPlU5xKhsXgiCrDr7kkyDds5uPNesw-6d2130406c50.png)

**Step 5:** Click the 'Downgrade' button to submit

![](/img/help/NNoeqVniwcN-e7xmRAWNH8gJvGubvFwqA-539657764c2d.png)
