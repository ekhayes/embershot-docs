**Step 1:** Click on the Account icon in the top right.

![](/img/help/zN0w2A6PzBT3lL_femVDZDkZSXA27nx4GA-1f28c6195ae8.png)

**Step 2:** From the dropdown, select 'Account Settings.'

![](/img/help/KE3tE1XQd3AIRbYbrzvb3AzRNYY5X8pWdQ-aa011e8f1996.png)

**Step 3:** Select 'Billing & Subscriptions.'

![](/img/help/HnUA5aGMK9BbI9DjKchKGlG9pw_VcGd_6w-960b9584a035.png)

**Step 4:** Click the 'Transaction History' button.

![](/img/help/Q71D4lyl_GdcsLBbQpm_8KFJrER4juQQXg-74557b452bf5.png)

**Step 5:** View 'Transaction History.'

![](/img/help/MkkaCZf0mYzKuXn2I5QgMAgqCoTzQ2BxpA-2e355b493b86.png)
