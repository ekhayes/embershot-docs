**Step 1:** Click on the Account icon in the top right.

![](/img/help/D2OtgoudXhUKmnLBPBF5JOQRA8HF6q0PEQ-866afbaafd04.png)

**Step 2:** From the dropdown, select 'Account Settings.'

![](/img/help/7CBXTILeUvrxwKccCzmvkOeQuo4EQKU5VA-02343d431029.png)

**Step 3:** Select 'Billing & Subscriptions.'

![](/img/help/ReEHSNc0B51EfOhYVy7QDv0AF6sZu1Vc2A-33755f36cfd4.png)

**Step 4:** Click the 'View Payment History' button.

![](/img/help/Jn08tx4sRLIRlJ86GenaZ0uiNsUHxA_dgg-e28fcf3efde9.png)

**Step 5:** Mouse over the transaction you wish to view and a 'Receipt' icon will appear. Click to the 'Receipt' icon to download and view the receipt.

![](/img/help/1LA32eEPVMqGCKkyapAW8Gta2PYfIfx6Hg-4b324870fd40.png)
