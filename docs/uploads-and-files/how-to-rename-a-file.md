**Step 1:** Click on the 'Settings' icon for the desired file.

![](/img/help/yEbkrQ9V_W-NhMfcXNclsk2fvUBxNe5gEQ-4434d2403313.png)

**Step 2:** Rename the file's title and click 'Update.' Click 'Close.'

![](/img/help/eTAS0pKtNRgcP6aJYMJL__bBtgXZn6Uz4A-986de06f0df3.png)
