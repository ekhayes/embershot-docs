**Step 1:** Click on the Account icon in the top right.

![](/img/help/xDh4sW5_X6tnDGioTu1KbCKp-se8mnQyw-3320c7f7b024.png)

**Step 2:** From the dropdown, select 'Account Settings.'

![](/img/help/z_KooDScamuVw4wQgN37mrnsyJ4kwp71Pw-a48dee6cc06a.png)

**Step 3:** Select 'Default Settings' from the menu on the left and adjust the desired 'Watermark Settings.' Click 'Close.'

![](/img/help/67BdQLCK0uQdFt2U7rgiAvbo_DtPZ3zOsQ-5d8ffd5960e4.png)

> This will be applied to all future uploaded files, not existing files in the account. If you do not edit a user's name, the system will default to what is in front of the @ symbol in the user's email address.
