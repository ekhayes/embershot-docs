**Step 1:** Click on the 'Settings' icon for the desired file.

![](/img/help/DVnR6MK49ljbk1Wc-HDzdR5K0obamol0ww-feb37e6ba4dc.png)

**Step 2:** Open the 'Watermark' dropdown.

![](/img/help/YFbIH-h5Hvd67rEnrwlFvHxpj8m8QscW_w-7486038115c1.png)

**Step 3:** Make the desired change to the watermark and then click 'Close'

![](/img/help/jM5G7-PcmEfdca8cPth91AzFUi1YAPhl6A-681283f2cce4.png)
