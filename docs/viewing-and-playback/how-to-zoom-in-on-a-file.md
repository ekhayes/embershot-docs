Click on the magnifying tool to zoom in on the file.

![](/img/help/YM6C7HWCIM8RcJTMHZCyHPU2sdXXwFvJKQ-9da4f02fafe8.png)

## IF ON CHROME BROWSER:

* Access the Chrome toolbar by clicking on the three dots in the top right. From the dropdown, click on the "+" on the Zoom tab in order to zoom in on the whole page.

![](/img/help/i3MOckpWpVKpS6q5DWWzxc21UzMI1HKG4A-5fcf1bfd4a67.png)

## IF ON COMPUTER:

* Press and hold "Command" and "+" **to zoom in.**
* Press and hold "Command" and "-" **to zoom out.**

## IF ON COMPUTER OR OTHER DEVICE WITH SMART PAD:

* **To zoom in**, press on the screen with your index finger and thumb, leaving a small amount of space between them. Keeping your finger and thumb on the screen, move them away from each other, expanding the space between them. As you expand your fingers, the screen zooms in.
* **To zoom out**, do the reverse. Move your thumb and index finger toward each other while keeping them pressed to the screen.
