**Step 1:** Go to [www.embershot.com](http://www.embershot.com/) and login to your account.

*Note: If you don't know your password, you can click the 'Forgot' button to reset your password. Just make sure you are logging in with the email that the file was sent to.*

![](/img/help/mCBrDXUEk1f2axXUO7kPshMHPzJyBhRWwg-f62784c57c5b.png)

**Step 2:** Click on the document image or title of the file you want to open
