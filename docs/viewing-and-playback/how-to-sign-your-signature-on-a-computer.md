---
If you are using a mouse: Click in the box, hold down the left-click button, and sign your name by drawing it with the mouse.

If you are using a trackpad: Click in the box, hold your index finger down on the trackpad, sign your name by drawing it within the box, and when you're finished, release the trackpad.
