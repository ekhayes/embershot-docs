---
**Step 1:** When viewing a document, click the menu in the top left corner.

![](/img/help/t2CPp8o3ozb_nn8DawY86QYBrNbrtLyGrA-6518c5f7e5d9.png)

**Step 2:** From the dropdown, select 'Options' and make sure the 'Lock View' toggle is on.

![](/img/help/pI59bmkRWaBD1XJATv5WAjiRcvzGLZYB6w-d1e9f0482941.png)
