**Step 1:** On Login Screen, click the menu.

![](/img/help/vF0K4uuIuwcy4HJ0OK2arTGouSVH-Y6k7w-972f5788e086.png)

**Step 2:** On the drop-down menu, click the button in the upper left corner. "Reset Data."

![](/img/help/hEGvDru-0FCVGGpNvsGr262Pw5QtEtv9fg-58cfd2472626.png)

**Step 3:** Click "Reset All"

![](/img/help/_i7LL449ExT75FtkmCSfRfbDFHpndY12uA-c16b697397b9.png)

**Step 4:** Click "Ok" and now you can log back in.

![](/img/help/c9cyvs3uP0vQm_icog8kpZaV2bvpOUxw1A-929e9c1c03ba.png)
