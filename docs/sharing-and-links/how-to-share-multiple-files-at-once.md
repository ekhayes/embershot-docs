**Step 1:** Select the files you wish to share by either clicking the 'Select All' checkbox, or by clicking the boxes that appear when hovering your cursor to the left of the files.

![](/img/help/sLk_LHE2duHEMK4edwEbjbbIGN7HGHrdsQ-5d9042af3424.png)

**Step 2:** Select 'Share Selected.'

![](/img/help/eg72uKKuTBA3UR9DC1WUPkgWaj9Dhsc26w-2bf3945b18ac.png)

**Step 3:** Fill out the forms and click 'Send.'

![](/img/help/QeniwYOfvNUHiGWhw7PoeMZJTlMU8arVcA-76a93d1bc195.png)
