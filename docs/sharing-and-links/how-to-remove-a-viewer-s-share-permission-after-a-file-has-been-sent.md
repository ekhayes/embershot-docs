---
1. Click on "Viewers".

![](/img/help/xk0aY_xquVW9V2m7d7mAQvjGt4LcP9Dqlg-06b10a00f1ac.png)

2. Click on the permissions and settings dropdown for the desired user.

![](/img/help/w_3g-eOZZMEyyCE6OvOtlKFEGcVKbH848A-9e05be7a28ee.png)

3. Select "Remove Share Permission".

![](/img/help/uWGRzvVG1Qu9kVBFwFuIIwTksGGLzHTYWw-0f2b274ce39d.png)
