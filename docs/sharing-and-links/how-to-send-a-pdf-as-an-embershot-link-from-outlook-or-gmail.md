The Embershot add-ins for Outlook and Gmail, and "Embershot link" in the Embershot app on iPhone and iPad, send a PDF as an Embershot Verified link instead of an attachment. Everyone on the email (To, Cc and Bcc) can open it with a one-time code sent to their own address, reads a copy watermarked with their email, and can't download or print it. You see who opened it and when. Available with Pro and Enterprise accounts.

## Install the add-in

* **Outlook:** your Microsoft 365 administrator can add it for everyone in your organization. To add it yourself, open 'Get Add-ins', choose 'My add-ins', then 'Add a custom add-in', and add `https://embershot.com/addin.ashx/outlook/manifest.xml` (if Outlook only offers 'Add from file', save that file first).
* **Gmail:** install 'Embershot' from the Google Workspace Marketplace.

## Connect the add-in (once)

**Step 1:** Open the add-in: in Outlook, start a new email and click 'Embershot' in the toolbar; in Gmail, start a new email and choose 'Embershot link' from the add-on icons at the bottom of the draft.

**Step 2:** Click 'Connect to Embershot'. A window opens.

**Step 3:** Sign in to Embershot if asked, then click 'Allow'.

## Send a PDF from Outlook

**Step 1:** Add the people you're sending to, and attach the PDF as usual.

**Step 2:** Click 'Embershot' in the toolbar. The add-in lists your recipients and your PDF attachments.

**Step 3:** Click 'Replace with Embershot links'. Each checked PDF becomes a link in your email, and the attachment is removed.

**Step 4:** Send the email.

When your administrator added the add-in for your organization, Outlook also asks, as you send an email with a PDF attached, whether to send it as an Embershot link. Choose 'Use Embershot', or send it as an attachment. You can turn this question off in the add-in.

## Send a PDF from Gmail

**Step 1:** Add the people you're sending to.

**Step 2:** Choose 'Embershot link' from the add-on icons at the bottom of the draft.

**Step 3:** Pick a PDF you sent before, or click 'Upload a PDF', choose the file and close the upload window when it's done.

**Step 4:** Click 'Insert Embershot link'. The link goes where your cursor was.

**Step 5:** Send the email.

## Send a PDF from your iPhone or iPad (any mail app)

**Step 1:** Open the PDF (in Mail, Files, Safari or any app) and tap Share.

**Step 2:** Choose Embershot in the row of apps. The first time, tap 'Connect to Embershot', sign in and tap 'Allow'.

**Step 3:** Type the email addresses of the people it's for, then tap 'Make Embershot link'.

**Step 4:** Tap 'Write email' to open a new email with the link and those people already in it, or 'Copy link' to paste it anywhere.

## Notes

* Only the people on the email when you make the link can open it. If you add someone afterwards, open the add-in again: in Outlook click 'Add new people to the link', in Gmail click 'Add people to the link only'.
* A forwarded link does not let anyone else in: a person who isn't on the list never gets a code.
* PDFs only, up to 100 MB.
* The PDFs you send this way are saved to your Embershot account.
* To disconnect, click 'Disconnect' in the Outlook add-in, 'Disconnect Embershot' in the Gmail add-on's menu, or 'Disconnect' in Embershot link on your iPhone. Changing your Embershot password or resetting two-step sign-in also disconnects it; connect again afterwards.
* Up to 100 people per email.
* If the add-in says links are turned off for your organization, ask your Embershot administrator or write to [support@embershot.com](mailto:support@embershot.com).
