**Step 1:** Click on 'Viewers' icon.

![](/img/help/XbY2DJ2bLn4KmJr_A4_xJijNCY_zoChoRg-6ec045138475.png)

**Step 2**: Click on the Share icon under Permissions column for the desired user.

![](/img/help/Xwtg7up4tBRiHt3XadE3gbu_AiLzhjSeRQ-131e9688d67b.png)

You can also access it in the Permissions dropdown menu

![](/img/help/hwB1fK6p1pCp4JyoIH8QYs8LvfKkmzg73A-b1b43846136f.png)

**Step 3:** Click on the Share Limit dropdown.

![](/img/help/BwonthkWuvhesABa13cIY9J3Ixv2P3633w-62d1545f4154.png)

**Step 4:** Select desired Share Limit.

![](/img/help/2O7S8CG8iSdvxU2UrPugYeFtVEWA1FukcQ-f45176407ef1.png)
