**Step 1:** Click 'Views'

![](/img/help/tX22pEx0K-0rGAmlt9OrScWsSHwDH5baCQ-528623dce53c.png)

**Step 2:** Notice the 'Action' next to the users' names

- Viewed
- Complete
- Deleted
- File Replaced
- Print-downloaded
- Upload

![](/img/help/Sa4hUp8a_UEkL6vNxyZ0owgryvlLBYoHsg-c4a4cd5e5c5d.png)

_Note: You can click on the 'Filter' dropdown to filter the History by different Actions_
