description: "What is the Average Progress?"
---
The Average Progress indicates the average percentage of how far your viewers got when viewing your file. For video and audio files it is a simple average of how far people get. For documents, it will show the average percentage of pages read by users. For example, if a user reads the first page and the last page of a 100-page document that means they only read 2% of the document and then we average that percentage across all users that have viewed the file.
