There are two ways to view a file's history.

**Option 1**

**Step 1:** Click on the 'Views' icon next to the file history you wish to observe

![](/img/help/DYCq1LBCz-B1tSSniLKyKZ-g925ZpChag-bb5a08d16c76.png)

**Step 2:** You will then see the history of all the users who have viewed the file

![](/img/help/XRJgbzaoMlh4OiHyK3VNaCbnKXnq05WCXw-acd45345b70a.png)

**Option 2**

**Step 1:** Click on the three dots 'More' menu next to the desired file.

![](/img/help/ETp5_wUjAy0WI73fwCs81NrTeEPHAyzIxQ-d723e78b059f.jpeg)

**Step 2:** Click on 'History.'

![](/img/help/KzsBOxnxZd7czAEYxhEgD74LgvON_2sqfA-1e696b91def3.jpeg)

**Step 3:** View the history of the file.

![](/img/help/dnmcwKZm5YktMnGGu7v0Wd7KcajEQHWY1g-958591b46842.png)
