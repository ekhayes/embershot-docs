**Step 1:** On the Home screen, click the three dots next to the project dropdown.

![](/img/help/GH7iwHjj0uUZgmbEP0bDxQUvzLUUL74IEQ-063836fe6e5f.png)

**Step 2:** "Select 'Project Analytics' from the menu."

![](/img/help/6GWHu2U_eGL_wbhRyRFHpfhW2mPsisZ1lg-e09b9adc584d.png)

**Step 3:** "Select 'Activity' to view overall project activity" and modify the date range using the dropdown menu in the bottom left corner.

![](/img/help/6W-Z-hGF4b5KW71N_9yK7VVc_L97Hx-cA-14dfca3a169c.png)

**Step 4:** "Select 'Top5' to view analytics for the 5 most active project files."

![](/img/help/jGGuGhpaDTqb8gUdWvB_R_kazlBm81d9BA-919a007ed9b2.png)
