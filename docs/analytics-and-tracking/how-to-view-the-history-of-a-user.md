**Step 1:** Click on the 'Groups' icon.

![](/img/help/v8-XDhxCdl2pbjIzNeimRYveWxwfrNNEVQ-b8865ca9afb1.png)

**Step 2:** Hover your cursor to the right of the desired user and click on the three dots.

![](/img/help/omiMBGM9TAKnMUT6__sNeyiotodcLCUD4g-5a0f8bccf62d.png)

**Step 3:** From the dropdown, click 'User History.'

![](/img/help/O5q3cVt_LkA8UK4MlUCBfM8P-Getk9heeQ-799eeecdce0f.png)

**Step 4:** View history in pop-up.

![](/img/help/CZmrwk4n3J6ImtmREty5sOnLqAwhJZ70lQ-7f9aa94e4cd4.png)
