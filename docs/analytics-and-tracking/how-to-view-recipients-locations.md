_**Users with an Enterprise account have access to recipient locations.**_ Recipient locations can be viewed in 4 ways.

## Option 1: VIEW LOCATIONS IN PROJECT HISTORY

**Step 1:** Click on the three dots 'More' menu next to the Projects dropdown.

![](/img/help/67Rpjn6ZRyk1P9QBrqqeb8cv_nSNO5Wcog-69857e2c84d9.png)

**Step 2:** From the dropdown, select 'Project History.'

![](/img/help/9_n5Sb1xMuYFuXMItDJYPuMF1dtnt1RCHQ-f2b3b6e2e35b.png)

**Step 3:** Look at the 'Location' column to see viewers' locations. You can also map the location by looking at 'Map View.'

![](/img/help/TViCcsdj78uCWAf8Ew2yyDLFy5TUlJ_U8A-60787de12c49.png)

_Note: Locations are approximated based on cell tower information._

## Option 2: VIEW LOCATIONS IN FILE HISTORY

**Step 1:** Click on the three dots 'More' menu next to the desired file.

![](/img/help/ZkFh7MXVpndP79_ASdTS_Ysz9ynkfsvzCw-5f19f28e490a.png)

**Step 2:** Click on 'History.'

![](/img/help/aUD7uvja1JxzSTWXHWIQOaOUMrRq2hyoJw-8eba6784a027.png)

**Step 3:** View the locations of all the recipients of the file.

![](/img/help/ZlK3t8xC0b-vTEqzq310Q4hEN0CB0pxfIw-ebec213ce740.png)

## Option 3: VIEW LOCATIONS IN USER HISTORY

**Step 1:** Open Groups & Users section.

**Step 2:** Click on the 'More' menu for the desired User.

![](/img/help/JRD3sFWPZFQNfEdRorMnCTMll1NSOvpQQA-ea22cdda470e.png)

**Step 3:** Select 'User History' from the dropdown menu.

![](/img/help/H2Z87cQ4aiovHllsf_Le36C7vHQotl1MWg-c2f3db2f9124.png)

**Step 4:** View the locations for all files viewed by the User.

![](/img/help/FCEB8T7JAsHDJT2N_AegpYhJlnNEjtnW1w-602777d3eaee.png)

## Option 4: VIEW LOCATIONS IN VIEWER HISTORY OF A SPECIFIC FILE

**Step 1:** Click the 'Viewers' icon for the desired file.

![](/img/help/2WGT8dhXx77S4W2HRZos9M7-RCwGa9mwTw-cbe2bb9a7ec2.png)

**Step 2:** Click the 'More' menu for the desired viewer.

![](/img/help/q7YKb8tnCFaMrgmZDI4T6_ehcanUJfsO-w-1252b323e6ca.png)

**Step 3:** Select 'View History' from the dropdown menu.

![](/img/help/FblhDG3Mf6JUEWtcavD2m-qcjEGwChzt7A-adca46c5f4a5.png)

**Step 4:** View the locations where the specified viewer engaged with the specific file.

![](/img/help/_f-6OrBnVq9DhRga6zPfO7pslPCjHXrAFQ-cd4430f6e57b.png)
