**Step 1:** Click on the Groups icon.

![](/img/help/Z-YSyKzL1M7M23cNH6aGp-eSZpcjkCUYBw-29c9b98053f6.png)

**Option 1:**

* Select "New User".

![](/img/help/pWw2YqbBiVbWBo5GZ35oLueMjUH5MAUVhA-a22cda2adb3b.png)

* Fill out the form fields and click "Add".

![](/img/help/r-5rb1-5wAR8pUAm7wY1F-hXT-EkGc8Ow-4a2e4810bcc7.png)

**Option 2:**

* Select "Add Bulk Users".

![](/img/help/ZplqTnfZYdKc_5p0Lx7ZvEKPNuIlYzADhw-bf4c9f6b2228.png)

* Fill out the form fields with multiple users' email addresses and click "Find Emails". Confirm users' addition to the group.

![](/img/help/XVC1NpP8I8exaQdjZ8ZH7O0_9UqO4GhGYA-c5df9e39a64b.png)
