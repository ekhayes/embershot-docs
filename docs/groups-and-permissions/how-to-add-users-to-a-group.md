**Step 1:** Click on the Groups icon.

![](/img/help/Z-YSyKzL1M7M23cNH6aGp-eSZpcjkCUYBw-29c9b98053f6.png)

**Step 2:** If you have not already, add [the desired users to the project](/groups-and-permissions/how-to-add-new-users-to-a-project) and [create the desired group](/groups-and-permissions/how-to-create-groups-in-a-project). Then click on the users you wish to add to the new group. When the users are selected, a teal highlight will appear around their names.

![](/img/help/Ow9HZao2w-_HvAHLIeoVF01FJGG7ZnLlHg-437b86eb844b.png)

**Step 3:** When all of the desired users have been selected, click on the "Add User" icon next to the group you wish to add the users.

![](/img/help/Vp6jv2P7SWydX6g-axSrBqm_5JX8YvPlWw-bf84de7cb372.png)

_Please note that you cannot add project admins to groups because they already have access to all of the project files_.

You can also [add users to groups from a files' viewer list.](https://help.embershot.com/en/groups-and-permissions/how-to-add-all-of-the-users-who-viewed-a-file-to-a-group-how-to-add-all-of-the-users-who-viewed-a-file-to-a-group)
