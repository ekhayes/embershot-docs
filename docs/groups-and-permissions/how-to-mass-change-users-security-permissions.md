**Step 1:** Go to the viewer list.

![](/img/help/_xTWCZi73VVMPXoH4JTqbEh3TvpY4ldRiA-2c0ae0ed5937.png)

**Step 2:** Select the users whose settings you wish to adjust

![](/img/help/aGNpkGRPxH1rRPRHQLsztxn7MkqRya0IfA-d92a31be629a.png)

**Step 3:** Click on the permission icon

![](/img/help/aUDLS7pvF-OsyV9w5loXGMRWp3Vu79Pf5g-a2931303665e.png)

**Step 4:** Adjust the desired permissions and click "Update Permissions"

![](/img/help/U31IvmrWpNSw6QOBrCJ-17hQiElW4yyUGw-02da7e8131cb.png)
