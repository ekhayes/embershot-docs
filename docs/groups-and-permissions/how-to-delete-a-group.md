**Step 1:** Click on 'Groups'

![](/img/help/9m0vaCDcUo1PwJ4PYIYfwMAGNgNK5eOQAQ-d1c5a62952da.png)

**Step 2:** Select the 'More' icon on desired group

![](/img/help/I1clYoyv9cAIgPaaTWMmKJS8wv6cA-uWxw-d7b5f6145043.png)

**Step 3:** Select 'Delete'

![](/img/help/H8q00TSNZs40NrLZTz8jks7-rSA-cY6wmg-430c8275769c.png)

**Step 4:** Confirm you want to delete that group by selecting 'Yes'

![](/img/help/4fk3jgYMJfXjJXu355xCMUq_jz0evfnYfw-705a9305c3a8.png)
