description: "How to grant access to a file after it has been revoked"
---
1. Click on "Viewers".

![](/img/help/Z8J-ZSb3ARXJDRfNJbjnJzBT9CiHhKir3w-150956dcbc7e.png)

2. Under "Status", click on the dropdown for the desired user and click on "Available". This should change from "Revoked" to "Available".

![](/img/help/LHKLHENCokqj6rvIMMc5XLLKINg1SsthRA-cc05ebb0897a.png)

3. This will prompt a calendar to open up. Select the date and time you intend the content to expire on and click "Save".

![](/img/help/SJV9RCNoKtEEosIYF_Y1ZfacS-Jipi1VYA-a9ab0e26052a.png)
