**Step 1:** Click on "Groups" icon.

![](/img/help/93aMbJkeCMRi04SWqveFrKYI0M0hZZsPdQ-57126aff242d.png)

**Step 2:** For the desired user, click on the three dots when hovering on their icon. From the dropdown, click 'Revoke Access to All Files' and confirm.

![](/img/help/3XkWxRCH2r2hg24RCtJN8yA3_lxazNOjFg-587475ab30a2.png)
