**Step 1:** Click on the desired file's viewer list icon

![](/img/help/IdXFWSVvlCVJNNA_dSdnRCMM9Ld9ioiy7g-cbb1586cb0eb.png)

**Step 2:** Apply your preferred filter

![](/img/help/FbZ3TEaZk4QwaXp1o1g71wEfEQOmASpEYg-0aa63b81fc45.png)

**Step 3:** Select the users you would like to add to a group

![](/img/help/wtZvTWFXw1AVR-bxsGF6F5YfMMe-e6lcUA-8fcf1644f994.png)

**Step 4:** Click on the "Add users to group" icon

![](/img/help/7tyFCdeCft-VqfEP2zMEetYULbHtpk_8tg-ad5749924fb7.png)

**Step 5:** Select the desired group to add the users to

![](/img/help/C2PNPPAddsuj-FTmzaJYDIUjf2H28SOWCQ-7e7d13752b4f.png)

You can also [add users to a group from the project user section.](/groups-and-permissions/how-to-add-users-to-a-group)
