---
**Step 1:** Click on the Account icon in the top right.

![](/img/help/l5wcEku9zUsLRXbcwrhHU__d79SESlUrVw-f160db0bffa7.png)

**Step 2:** From the dropdown, select 'Account Settings.'

![](/img/help/gtABrTTNhrUi5N0HyLlMLnBZsLTwo9-KAg-168db24fe675.png)

**Step 3:** Select 'User Profile' from the left and input your 'Mobile #.' Click 'Update' and save any changes.

![](/img/help/B9BUl7rDZFTKRXT_iPppP-YcLPuQW12zzw-bda8a9d72e69.png)
