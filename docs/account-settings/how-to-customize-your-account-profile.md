**Step 1:** Select the account icon in the top right corner.

![](/img/help/oy7jWR0pUDMu3olGiGz-YzfFMjMJWSFI8w-ca35538f8af7.png)

**Step 2:** Select 'Account Settings' from the dropdown menu.

![](/img/help/HKJvVvagLg_F5AuYpojGQHgg4x34zjyGdA-db041ccd07c8.png)

**Step 3:** Go to the 'User Profile' section

![](/img/help/KsO6oyuFx4_TrBcf_feZPAjby96B_h1K3g-ecbf3b186f4f.png)

**Step 4:** Add a profile photo, edit your 'Display Name,' and add additional contact information.

*(Note: The account 'Username' CANNOT be edited, as it is the email address/mobile # used to create the account.)*

![](/img/help/t0wSWjug5wPHbVjKk2-Vvp5QDxX1BlHxaA-cd01a6445f64.png)
