**Step 1:** On the login screen or in the file view, click the Menu icon.

![](/img/help/OhX1b7Qv-N9QWWm6DQjjWOopyhfQJedZUA-7ae344a7d5df.png)

**Step 2:** In the drop-down menu, click "Contact Us."

![](/img/help/HTYa6Gk6G7mB7PGPsToc7Zq9HC4kERWEng-af0298ea0c4b.png)

**Step 3:** Type in your contact info, the description of the error, check the "Report Error" box and hit "Send."

![](/img/help/KrDfCtOP9HkUrHLfdUSGV5zxSBQ084dVfA-1f472a9028fb.png)
