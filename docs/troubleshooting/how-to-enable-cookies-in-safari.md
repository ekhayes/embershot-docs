---
**Step 1:** Within Safari, click the 'Safari' menu and choose 'Preferences.'

![](/img/help/fq5fCbjAjSsbSG9S3DVLwOnIxh2eNx4juw-63d3101f89ec.png)

**Step 2:** Select the 'Privacy' icon in the pop-up window. To block all cookies, check the 'Block all cookies' box. To allow all cookies, leave it unchecked.

![](/img/help/7u4EXqQEa3QXfQcHlGG9aX0e5unyXf5_XQ-c9cd25dfb2a6.png)
