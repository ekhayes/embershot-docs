There are 2 ways to report a bug on a browser.

**OPTION 1**

**Step 1:** Click the Profile menu

![](/img/help/kA7nItJ-Kbj9Y0eyx4zsNukqgEjpbGNxlQ-5d04421ff15b.png)

**Step 2:** Select 'Contact Embershot'

![](/img/help/O_6w-OLXoOmcM9sWh9id_Q9VpnQ_Or-QvQ-a536539ecfa2.png)

**Step 3:** "Your contact info associated with the account will automatically pre-fill. Select the 'Report Bug' box and press 'Send' to submit."

![](/img/help/SIZO_OU8WT3cTfT4i5PLyeuPMmjO_LXFIA-1d4e405462c4.png)

**OPTION 2**

**Step 1:** When viewing a file, click the menu in the top left corner.

![](/img/help/4j0hKsvbfgUybbFgopzIAFXD6quvQMBBiQ-e7e8f51d76f6.png)

**Step 2:** From the dropdown, click 'Contact Us.'

![](/img/help/XDGPiX_K7OmgG3Rr3SOKNyjwkNvUSVnW5g-702d1b30eb9e.png)

**Step 3:** "Fill out the form. Be sure to check 'Report Error' and then click 'Send.'"

![](/img/help/AteWVL5i1POWhEDj9OniUpYZCQxxLbM55g-cd8deaf28119.png)
