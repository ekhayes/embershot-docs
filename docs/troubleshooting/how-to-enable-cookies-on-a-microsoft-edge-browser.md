description: "How to enable cookies on a Microsoft Edge browser"
---
**Step 1:** Click the three dot 'More' menu in the top right and from the dropdown, select 'Settings.'

**Step 2:** Click 'Site permissions' and then 'Cookies and site data.'

**Step 3:** Select "Allow sites to save and read cookie data" to enable cookies.
