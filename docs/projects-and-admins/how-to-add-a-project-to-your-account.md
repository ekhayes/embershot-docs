To add a project to your Embershot account, click the green 'Create Project' button in the top right of your [Pro Account](https://embershot.freshdesk.com/en/account-billing/how-to-upgrade-to-a-pro-account-with-a-project-how-to-upgrade-to-a-pro-account-with-a-project).

![](/img/help/ym4oOvdF42eay0TpXXv0SdiVo15OpKG2ng-e38d429f5901.png)

Please note that "only Pro Users with paying accounts can create a project." If you're part of an organization with an Enterprise Pro account, contact your site administrator to request project creation.
