**Step 1:** On the Home screen, click the three dots next to the project dropdown.

![](/img/help/Fvvf74mbjzzXJhkiKCUU6qnEtRGBwkkgXg-a99db597b343.png)

**Step 2:** From the resulting dropdown, select "Add/Edit Project NDA".

![](/img/help/uHJQflKFwSESsWYnC22cX0r75mn-3m2iCQ-13d2682097b2.png)

**Step 3:** Fill out the text box with your terms and click "Save".

![](/img/help/NjJcuU0H58Xe5mst6NLkPirUUGtmT1V5kw-a0d2fc756205.png)

When you are ready to share a file with terms on your project, your project terms will automatically load on the share panel.

![](/img/help/NNBvoT8a1Q9rUjED_V-0HcpP9EhosgT8ug-43897dd16bc5.png)
