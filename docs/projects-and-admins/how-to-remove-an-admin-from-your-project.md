_There are 2 options available:_

**Option 1: Revoke Admin Access**

**Step 1:** Click on the 'Groups' icon.

![](/img/help/Bu2Ob_5plfPRxM5p5M-jCktPD5gmN_Xz3A-0dc482b9b100.png)

**Step 2:** Hover your cursor to the right of the user you wish to demote and click on the three dot User menu.

![](/img/help/qO7pLy0BHDGeYQLf66jMcSpeyOjxNFJGpw-0ded736d5bb4.png)

**Step 3:** From the dropdown, click 'Remove Admin Access.'

![](/img/help/fPciGwQ_pNkBLlVDMlVuCz2VFY7LFACuYA-b36ef1b94de1.png)

**Option 2: Delete the user from your account**

**Step 1:** From the dropdown, click 'Remove from Project.'

![](/img/help/ufU7-oF5FlOaKsWs1mH61JQdzpPeUTM6Cw-940e1d27d9f9.png)
