---
**Step 1:** On the Home screen, click the three dots next to the project dropdown.

![](/img/help/U3rxrXoPhpsajAvu5iZOVzSSK7P0aEbu_w-ae2a70fcdd59.png)

**Step 2:** Select 'Upload Project Logo' from the menu, then choose and upload the desired logo file from your device.

![](/img/help/PwqqyI_6H6oup8XcTLM4m1u3xx664Ysn7g-b54fa98586aa.png)

**Step 3:** The new project logo appears next to the project name in the 'Projects' dropdown.

![](/img/help/NWjx-Mxef1ET0jhcrzLJkBP1dWiDDyvGQw-5f806dd0db0b.png)
