---
**Step 1:**  On the Home screen, click the three dots next to the project dropdown.

![](/img/help/OExTnEyTl1CCloSA0uWd82DYSTaEMCFtiQ-07869894a612.jpeg)

**Step 2:**  Select 'Deactivate Project' from the menu.

![](/img/help/iOn5AmWYhxEND8DktA-EWjphy6wC6ybcvA-1fa49a6fc54e.png)

**Step 3:**  Confirm you want to deactivate the project by clicking "Yes."

![](/img/help/TDAv61ZtbuI3F_X2nxSG0Epq4les8piX3A-9160e9eca57f.png)
